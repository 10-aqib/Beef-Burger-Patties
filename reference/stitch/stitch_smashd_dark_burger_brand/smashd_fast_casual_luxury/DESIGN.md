## Brand & Style
The design system powers an unapologetic, visceral, high-octane digital storefront for a premium smash burger house. It merges brutalist structural precision with sensory, cinematic indulgence—translating the intense heat of a 450°F cast-iron flat-top and the sizzle of lace-edged beef patties into a digital ecosystem. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Tactile High-Contrast Noir with Street-Food Luxury**:
- **Personality**: Energetic, precise, unapologetic, hungry, and sophisticated.
- **Audience**: Urban trendsetters, culinary purists, late-night high-rollers, and premium fast-casual diners in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad demanding frictionless ordering and peerless culinary quality.
- **Emotional Response**: Immediate salivation, raw excitement, and total confidence in gastronomic craftsmanship.
- **Visual Stance**: Pitch-black canvas layers, razor-sharp geometric framing, cinematic embers, molten gradients, and high-impact editorial typography.

## Colors
The palette is built on obsidian depths, cast-iron charcoals, and volatile thermo-gradients mimicking searing heat and caramelized crusts.

- **Obsidian Core (Backgrounds)**: `#080808` defines the bottom canvas, layered with `#0D0D0D` for primary surface backdrops.
- **Charcoal Containers**: `#171717` (Level 1 Surface) and `#1F1F1F` (Level 2 Surface) provide elevated containment for food cards, trays, and interactive sheets.
- **Borders & Micro-Dividers**: `#2A2A2A` creates surgical boundary lines; `#383838` provides focus states.
- **Searing Heat (Primary Accents)**: `#FF3B1F` drives core CTAs, badge labels, and dynamic cart counters. Its secondary tint, `#FF6B4A`, provides active states and glow envelopes.
- **Molten Amber (Highlights)**: `#F59E0B` represents melted aged cheddar, toasted brioche, and premium upsells (truffle glaze, signature brisket blends, and VIP perks).
- **Text & Foreground**: Full-cream `#FFFFFF` delivers crisp legibility against dark surfaces; `#A3A3A3` provides muted editorial metadata and nutritional annotations; `#737373` designates disabled states.
- **Currency & Commercial Signifiers**: All price tags are consistently paired with `Rs.` in high-contrast primary or full white.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy balances muscular brutalist display headers with frictionless transactional utility:

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its industrial ink traps, geometric aggression, and architectural stance. Headlines must be capitalized strategically for signature burgers, combative slogans, and heat gauges.
- **Body & Operations (Inter)**: Handles descriptive ingredients, nutritional breakdowns, modifier choices, and operational checkout lines with zero-distortion legibility.
- **Labels & Tags (Space Grotesk Uppercase)**: Used across badge elements (e.g., `DOUBLE SMASH`, `HOT`, `RS. 1,450`, `WAGYU SPECIAL`). High tracking enhances tactical urgency.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column dynamic grid system anchored by structural precision:

- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Sticky bottom actions for fast single-thumb ordering, sticky quick-category anchor navigation.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Dual-pane layout for product visualization and customizers.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max container width of 1280px (`80rem`), 24px gutters. Visual heavy left-column editorial spreads with persistent right-column checkout tray.
- **Rhythm**: Everything is structured on a 4px/8px strict baseline grid, maintaining tight industrial density without sacrificing editorial whitespace around product photography.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through low-light atmospheric contrast, hard-edge surface layering, and thermal luminescence rather than soft diffuse white-world dropshadows:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base`: `#080808` canvas.
  - `Layer 1 (Cards, Menu Rows)`: `#171717` bounded by 1px `#2A2A2A` perimeter borders.
  - `Layer 2 (Modals, Slide-over Cart, Dropdowns)`: `#1F1F1F` with 1px `#383838` edge highlights.
- **Thermal Glows (Embers & Sizzle)**: High-order interactive components (selected items, Primary Add to Cart) feature a localized amber-crimson rim glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 59, 31, 0.35), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 59, 31, 0.6)`.
- **Glass Overlays**: Top navigation headers and floating cart strips utilize frosted darkness (`rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and 1px border-bottom `#2A2A2A`).

## Shapes
The design embraces an architectural, near-brutalist softness (`Level 1`):
- Standard elements (Cards, Buttons, Inputs, Panels) employ a sharp `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Pill shapes are strictly forbidden for primary action buttons to prevent a playful, candy-like aesthetic; buttons must feel solid, heavy, and press-stamped.
- Minor tag pills and allergen icons may utilize subtle edge softening, but the overarching architecture remains blocky, chiseled, and unapologetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("SMASH ORDER / ADD TO BAG")**: Solid `#FF3B1F` background, stark `#FFFFFF` bold uppercase text, 4px corner radius, padding `16px 28px`. Hover: `#FF543B` with thermal rim glow. Active: scale 0.98.
- **Secondary / Modifier CTA**: Background `#171717`, border 1px `#2A2A2A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: border `#F59E0B`, text `#F59E0B`.
- **Destructive / Remove**: Ghost style, text `#A3A3A3`, hover text `#FF3B1F`.

### Product & Menu Cards
- Background `#171717`, 1px border `#2A2A2A`, radius 6px.
- High-contrast edge-to-edge cinematic burger imagery lit with amber-rim key lighting.
- Floating price tag in top-right or bottom-right: `#0D0D0D` badge with border `#383838`, Space Grotesk text in `#F59E0B` (`Rs. 1,290`).
- Hover state raises card with 1px border transition to `#FF3B1F` and image subtle zoom (1.03 scale).

### Customizer Chips & Modifier Selectors
- Single/Multi-select patty, cheese, and sauce selectors.
- Default: `#1F1F1F` background, `#737373` label, 1px `#2A2A2A` border.
- Selected: `#FF3B1F` border, `#FFFFFF` text, with micro ember indicator dot `#FF3B1F` on the left.

### Form Inputs & Delivery Details
- Inputs feature background `#0D0D0D`, border 1px `#2A2A2A`, font `Inter 15px`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#525252`.
- Focus state: border 1px `#FF3B1F`, subtle glow `0 0 0 2px rgba(255, 59, 31, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom sharp square boxes (4px radius) for modifiers.
- Checked state: Filled `#FF3B1F` with black checkmark icon; border `#FF3B1F`.

### Food Customizer Drawer & Cart Tray
- Dark charcoal slide-over panel `#121212`, 1px border-left `#2A2A2A`.
- Fixed bottom sticky bar containing real-time price calculator: `Rs. [Total]` in `price-hero` style and high-impact full-width checkout action.
- Free delivery progress bar calibrated in Pakistani Rupees (e.g., `Add Rs. 450 more for Free Priority Delivery`).
