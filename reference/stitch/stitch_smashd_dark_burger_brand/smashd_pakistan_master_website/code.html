CRAFT PHILOSOPHY

WE TAKE BURGERS DEADLY SERIOUSLY.

SMASHD was born out of frustration with dry, thick, flavorless pub patties drowned in cheap condiments. We believed Pakistan deserved authentic street-level Maillard perfection.

We grind our proprietary blend of fresh brisket and chuck every single dawn across Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi. We never freeze. We never microwave. When your ticket prints, an iron press drops on high-heat flat steel to create that unmistakable shatter-crisp rim you crave.
